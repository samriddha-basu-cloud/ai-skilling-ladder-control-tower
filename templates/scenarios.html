How to read them together

"Today's system" is the floor: it is roughly what happens with no new intervention. "Targets at their floors" is a warning, not a plan: it shows the published targets don't add up to VACR 40% by themselves unless downstream stages are almost perfect. "Operating plan with buffer" is what the team actually proposes running, sitting above the floors with headroom to absorb normal slippage. "Stretch plan" is the ceiling if every intervention performs at its best observed rate. Use the Pilot simulator's preset buttons to switch between them live, or the Compare scenarios chart to see all four side by side.
