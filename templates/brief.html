Executive brief | AI Skill Ladder | Team Vertex | Scenario: {{ active_scenario.name }} | {{ now }}

India has built the AI-learning rails. Now build the bridge from learning to verified work.

A national learning-to-earning architecture that converts AI access into verified capability, workplace application and career mobility, measured by one North Star and scaled only on evidence.

1. The problem is conversion, not access

Access is at scale: {{ H.yuva.value }} YUVA AI for ALL enrolments {{ src(['S09']) }}, 34 lakh+ FutureSkills PRIME registrations and {{ H.sidh.value }} SIDH candidates {{ src(['S01','S02']) }}. But FSP certifies 58% of enrolees and SOAR about 20% {{ src(['S01','S02']) }}, and no public release reports verified competence, placement or productivity. Only {{ H.naps_ai.value }} apprentices have trained in AI roles under NAPS-2 {{ src(['S04']) }}. Demand is real: GenAI could transform 38 mn organised-sector jobs by 2030 {{ src(['S23']) }}.

Five failure modes break the chain: {% for f in fms %}{{ f.id }} {{ f.name }}{% if not loop.last %}; {% endif %}{% endfor %}. Root cause: schemes pay for seats (PMKVY 17.7% placed) while outcome funding shows what is possible (Skill Impact Bond 73% joined jobs) {{ src(['S26','S28']) }}.

2. The solution: a six-level ladder and seven interventions on existing rails

L0 Awareness to L5 Leader/Governor, each assessed on technical, non-technical and responsible AI. Interventions: {% for i in ints %}{{ i.id }} {{ i.title }}{% if not loop.last %}; {% endif %}{% endfor %}. Every step writes evidence to the AI Skill Passport; AI runs the system, humans make every high-stakes call.

3. Prove it: ₹290 Cr, 12 to 18 months, seven states {{ tag('ass') }}

Tamil Nadu and Andhra Pradesh (volume), Karnataka and Odisha (conversion), Uttar Pradesh (scale, North), Bihar and Assam (catch-up, North-East), each with a comparison district. In the active scenario, {{ '{:,}'.format(sim.params.entrants) }} entrants yield {{ '{:,}'.format(sim.verified) }} verified converts: VACR {{ k.vacr }}%, cost ₹{{ '{:,}'.format(k.cost_per_convert or 0) }} per convert, about {{ sim.econ.months_of_earnings }} months of average urban salaried earnings {{ src(['S30']) }}. Five-year envelope ₹6,500 Cr; central share {{ env.central_pct_msde }}% of MSDE's FY27 budget a year {{ src(['S11']) }}. No manufactured ROI.
